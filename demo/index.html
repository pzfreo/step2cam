An experiment in explainable planning, not CAM to run on a machine: it does not model workholding, tolerances or cutting forces, and every tool and feed is a placeholder. Runs in your browser: your file is not uploaded. Visits and actions (a sample, a file opened, play, save, a tab or Why opened) are counted with GoatCounter: no cookies, no identifier, and nothing about your file beyond whether it planned or a short error message. To see whether people come back, once a day the page counts, in broad groups, on how many days it has been used in this browser and how long ago it first was; that tally stays in your browser. step2cam uses specify-core, camlib (MIT), three-cad-viewer and three.js. PDF downloads use jsPDF 2.5.2 (MIT), AutoTable 3.8.4 (MIT), and DejaVu Sans (font licence), fetched from jsDelivr only when requested.
