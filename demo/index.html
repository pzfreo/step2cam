STEP2CAM

An experiment, not CAM to run on a machine. step2cam drafts a plan for simple parts and shows why it made each choice. It does not model workholding, tolerances or cutting forces, and every tool and feed is a placeholder. Have someone who knows machining check anything before it is cut.

Open a STEP file, or drop one on the page. It is processed in your browser, not uploaded.
